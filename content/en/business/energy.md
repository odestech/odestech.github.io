### Energy, Chemicals & Refining Additives

We serve refining, petrochemical and energy engineering projects with import/export and supply-chain services covering both process chemicals and plant hardware:

1.  **Refining Additives & Process Chemicals**: Catalysts and co-catalysts, corrosion inhibitors, antiscalants, demulsifiers, defoamers, desulfurisation and deNOx agents, fuel and lubricant additives, cooling water and process water treatment chemicals.
2.  **Process Plant Components**: Valves (gate, ball, butterfly, control, pressure relief), pump and compressor spares, mechanical seals and gaskets, pipe fittings and flanges, heat exchanger tube bundles and static equipment parts, instrumentation and automation components.
3.  **Compliance & Technical Services**: SDS/GHS authoring, UN dangerous goods classification and export packaging certification; market access support for REACH and the US TSCA inventory; EN 10204 3.1/3.2 material certificates; specification review against API 6D, API 610, API 682 and ASME B16.5.
4.  **Supply Chain Integration**: Qualified supplier development and factory audits, third-party inspection (SGS / BV / TÜV), consolidated procurement and spare-parts consignment, and cross-border logistics for both hazardous and general cargo.
