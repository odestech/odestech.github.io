## How We Work

1. **Enquiry** — product name or CAS number, specification, quantity, destination port and Incoterms.
2. **Sourcing** — we match the specification against qualified manufacturers rather than resell whatever is in stock.
3. **Quotation** — a complete enquiry is normally quoted within one business day.
4. **Documentation** — SDS and GHS labelling, dangerous goods classification and packaging certification, material certificates, certificates of origin.
5. **Inspection and shipment** — third-party inspection where required, then cross-border logistics for hazardous or general cargo.
