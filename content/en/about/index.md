**ODES** — Shanghai Odes Technology Co., Ltd. (上海奥得斯科技有限公司) — is a holding company focused on China–US trade and cross-border industrial collaboration.

Based in Shanghai and facing the United States and other high-standard overseas markets, we connect China's manufacturing and technical capability with buyers who need a counterparty on the ground: one that can qualify a factory, read a specification, and produce the documents an importer's customs broker will ask for.

## What We Do

International trade and technical services across two sectors — pharmaceuticals and life sciences, and energy, chemicals and materials. In practice that means supplier qualification and factory audits, specification review against the standards a buyer cites, export documentation and dangerous goods handling, third-party inspection, and consolidated shipping for both hazardous and general cargo.

## Core Values

* **O**verseas — built for buyers outside China, in their documentation standards
* **D**edicated — a named counterpart per account, not a shared inbox
* **E**xcellent — specification-level review before a quotation goes out
* **S**ynergistic — long-term supply relationships rather than one-off trades
