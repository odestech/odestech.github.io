{{/* layouts/shortcodes/business-card.html One sector card. `img` is a path under assets/, not a URL under static/, so Hugo can derive sized versions of it. The cards sit side by side, so every thumbnail is cropped to the same 16:10 box; otherwise a portrait-ish photo next to a landscape one leaves the two cards at different heights. A raster source is cropped by Hugo and shipped as WebP with a srcset, with a JPEG left as the fallback src. An SVG is passed through untouched, since Hugo cannot resize vectors, and .business-card-img crops it to the same box in CSS. */}} {{- $title := .Get "title" -}} {{- $subtitle := .Get "subtitle" -}} {{- $href := .Get "href" -}} {{- $img := .Get "img" -}} {{- $res := "" -}} {{- with $img -}} {{- $res = resources.Get . -}} {{- if not $res -}} {{- errorf "business-card: no asset %q.
